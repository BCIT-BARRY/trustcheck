Know Your Answers.

What BC teams ask before their first check

Who is TrustCheck built for?

Canadian banks, credit unions, fintechs and online services that need to verify customer identities and keep a record of every result.

What customer details do I submit?

First and last name, date of birth, address, country, document type and document number.

Which ID documents are supported?

Passports and driver’s licences. Each check records the document type and number.

What do the statuses mean?

Submitted means received. Verifying means TrustCheck is processing it. Completed means a result is ready.

Can I see all my verifications in one place?

Yes. List every verification with its current status, result and reason through the TrustCheck API.
